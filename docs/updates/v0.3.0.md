# clawshell v0.3.0

clawshell now speaks Arabic, Russian and Simplified Chinese as well as English and Persian, can show dates in your own calendar, and lets you copy and quit Claude sessions from an account's menu.

## New

### More languages

Arabic, Russian and Simplified Chinese join English and Persian. The language picker shows each language in its own name, Arabic is laid out right to left, and the tray menu and desktop notifications are translated too. On first run, clawshell starts in your Windows display language when it is one of these, and in English otherwise. A language you have already chosen is never changed.

### Calendar setting

Under Language, pick **Calendar**: Automatic, Gregorian, Jalali, Hijri or Chinese lunar. It decides how dates are written and which day the heatmap's weeks start on. Numbers keep the digits of your language. Automatic picks Jalali for Persian, Hijri for Arabic and Gregorian otherwise.

### Copy sessions

**Copy sessions…** in an account's context menu lists its Code sessions from every organisation and copies the ones you tick into another account. Archived sessions stay archived, and a session the target already has is replaced.

### Quit Claude per account

**Quit Claude** in a running account's context menu ends all of that account's Claude processes, including one hidden in its tray.

## Changed

- "This month" follows your calendar, and the heatmap dates and limit notifications do too.
- Account rows, the usage card and the heatmap show placeholders while they first load, and they keep their size when the numbers arrive.
- Dragging an account moves the rows live. The default account can be moved like any other.
- Rename and badge colour are one **Edit account** item.

## Fixed

- An account's status badges stay on its name's row when the email is long.

## For maintainers

- Release builds create signed updater artifacts and upload `latest.json` with the installers. The workflow fails early if the signing key or the updater public key is missing.
- See [Releasing](../../README.md#releasing) for the signing key setup.
